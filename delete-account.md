# Delete your Elowa account

You can delete your Elowa account and all data linked to it at any time.

## In the app

1. Open Elowa and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## By email

If you can no longer open the app, email **contact@webdesignsun.com** from the email address linked to your Elowa account (the Google account you sign in with) with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your profile and profile photo
- Children's profiles and their photos
- All stories, illustrations and audio narration
- Cloned voices, including their copies at our voice provider ElevenLabs
- Listening and reading progress, and your Sparks balance

## What may be kept

- Subscription and purchase records may be kept for up to the period required by accounting and tax law. After deletion they are no longer linked to your profile.
- Server logs are kept for a limited period, typically no more than 90 days.

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription. Cancel it in your store settings first.
