# Elowa Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Elowa mobile application (the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The App is operated by Web Design Sun, a company registered in Spain ("we", "us", "our"). We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Elowa accounts are for **adults aged 18 and over**. Parents and caregivers use the App to create stories for their children. Any information about a child is entered by the adult account holder.

## 1. Data we collect

### 1.1 Account data
- **Google sign-in data:** your name, email address and Google account identifier, received from Google when you sign in.
- **Profile:** the display name and optional profile photo you set.

### 1.2 Children's profiles (entered by you)
- Child's **first name**
- **Age group** (2–3, 4–5, 6–8 or 9–12). We do not ask for a date of birth.
- **Interests**, chosen from a fixed list (for example "animals" or "space")
- Optional **photo**, taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library

We do not collect a child's gender, exact date of birth, location or contact details.

### 1.3 Story content
- Story settings you choose: genre, length, visual style, moral, character names, appearance and abilities, and any free-text idea you type.
- The stories generated for you: text, illustrations and audio narration.
- Listening and reading progress: last page, completed or not, audio or text mode.

### 1.4 Voice recordings and cloned voices
- When you create a narration voice, the App records a **30–40 second sample of your voice** using your microphone.
- The sample is sent over an encrypted connection to our voice provider, ElevenLabs, to create a synthetic voice.
- **We do not store the recording on our servers.** We keep only the voice name you choose and the identifier of the synthetic voice.
- A synthetic voice model may be considered biometric data in some jurisdictions. We create one only at your request and with your consent, use it only to narrate your stories, and delete it when you ask.

### 1.5 Purchases and credits
- Subscription plan, status, renewal and expiry dates, and the store transaction identifier.
- Subscription event history received from RevenueCat.
- Sparks balance and usage counters.
- **We never receive your payment card details.** Payments are processed entirely by Apple or Google.

### 1.6 Technical data
- Session tokens stored on your device to keep you signed in.
- Cached images, such as avatars and illustrations, stored on your device to speed up the App.
- Server logs created when the App calls our backend: timestamps, request status and error details.
- The RevenueCat SDK collects device and app information needed to validate purchases, such as platform, OS version, app version and an anonymous app user ID.

We do **not** use advertising identifiers, do not show ads, do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 2. How we use data

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (where GDPR applies) |
|---|---|---|
| Create and manage your account | Account data | Performance of contract |
| Generate personalized stories, illustrations and narration | Children's profiles, story content, cloned voice | Performance of contract; your consent for voice cloning |
| Show your library and progress | Story content, progress | Performance of contract |
| Process subscriptions, restore purchases, manage Sparks | Purchases and credits | Performance of contract |
| Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix errors | Technical data, logs | Legitimate interests |
| Answer support requests | Data you send us by email | Legitimate interests |
| Comply with legal obligations | As required | Legal obligation |

We do **not** sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 3. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the App, and only as much as each one needs:

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (hosting, database, file storage, authentication) | All data described in Section 1 | Store and serve your account and content |
| **Google** (Google Sign-In) | Sign-in request | Authenticate you |
| **Apple / Google Play** | Purchase data | Process payments and subscriptions |
| **RevenueCat** | Your Elowa user ID, purchase and subscription data, device and app information | Validate purchases and manage subscription status |
| **OpenAI** | Story settings, including character names you enter, the child's **first name and age**, and your free-text idea | Generate story text and illustration descriptions |
| **ElevenLabs** | Your voice recording, the voice name, your Elowa user ID as a label, and story text | Create your synthetic voice and narrate stories |
| **Replicate** | Text descriptions of illustrations, which may include character names or descriptions | Generate illustrations |

- **Children's photos and interests are not sent to AI providers.**
- These providers act as our processors or service providers. They may process data only under their own terms and data-protection commitments.
- We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a transfer of the App to a new owner who agrees to this Policy.

## 4. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries outside your own. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses offered by those providers.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Account, children's profiles, stories and progress:** until you delete them or delete your account.
- **Cloned voices:** until you delete the voice or your account. Deletion also removes the voice from ElevenLabs.
- **Voice recordings:** not stored on our servers. A temporary copy may remain in the App's temporary storage on your device until the operating system clears it.
- **Subscription event records:** up to [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 7 years] after the event, to meet accounting and tax obligations and to handle disputes. After account deletion these records are no longer linked to your profile.
- **Server logs:** a limited period set by our hosting provider, typically no more than 90 days.

## 6. Deleting your data

- **Delete a voice:** Profile → Voices → delete.
- **Delete your account:** Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, children's profiles, photos, stories, audio, illustrations and cloned voices.
- Deleting your account does **not** cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your store settings (see our Terms of Service).
- You can also request deletion by emailing **contact@webdesignsun.com** from the email address linked to your account.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
- **correct** inaccurate data;
- **delete** your data;
- **restrict** or **object to** certain processing;
- **data portability**;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, for example by deleting a cloned voice, without affecting processing before withdrawal;
- **lodge a complaint** with your local data-protection authority; in Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).

**California residents:** we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the service.

To exercise these rights, email **contact@webdesignsun.com**. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, usually within 30 days, and may need to verify your identity first.

## 8. Children's privacy

- Elowa accounts are not intended for children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account.
- Information about children is provided by their parent or caregiver, who controls it and can edit or delete it at any time.
- We collect only what is needed to personalize stories: first name, age group, interests and an optional photo.
- If you believe a child has created an account or given us personal data directly, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

- We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls that separate each user's data, and restricted server-side keys for third-party services.
- No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
- If a data breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

## 10. Device permissions

- **Microphone:** used only while you record a voice sample.
- **Camera and photo library:** used only when you choose a profile photo for yourself or a child.
- You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. The related features will stop working, but the rest of the App will continue to work.

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will change the "Effective date" above and, for material changes, notify you in the App before they take effect.
